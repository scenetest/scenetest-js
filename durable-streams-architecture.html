“Is ② a StreamDB?”

StreamDBTheir own reactive DB on the State Protocol — a sibling to TanStack DB. A fork in the road, not a layer. On the TanStack path you don't use it.
② factoryThe TanStack integration: a *CollectionOptions function. This is what replaces the old facade.

Why this shape

Maintainers already maintain two of these. A third that conforms to the same convention and is the simplest of the three (read-only) is a small, legible ask — not a new abstraction.

Honest tradeoff

A WS tail gives up CDN-cacheability & request-collapsing — each socket is stateful upstream. Pitch it as a Cloudflare deployment transport, not a protocol-core change.

Decisive unknown

How factored is the stream client's transport today? That single fact = upstream PR vs thin fork of the client (never ②). Next: read the @durable-streams client source, find the seam.

Read top→bottom: data flows up from the log; you depend down. Green is almost everything. The new surface is one transport (⑥/④) and, if upstream won't take the seam, a thin client fork (③) — plus a factory (②) that merely conforms to an existing TanStack convention.
