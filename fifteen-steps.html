What swaps · what doesn't

Swaps (5–9): connect, fan-out, framing, decode — the pipe.

Never moves: log+offset (3·4), resume (10), your Transport/source (11·12), verbs (2·13), schema (14·15).

Who owns the pipe

partyserverserver 5·6·7 — fan-out, framing, hibernation
partysocketclient 8 — connect + reconnect (WS)
EventSourceclient 8 — the SSE peer of partysocket

They do not own 3·4 (log) or 10 (resume) — those are Durable-Streams-shaped, built on the DO's storage.

Is EventSource “our noun”?

No — it's the browser's SSE client primitive, the peer of partysocket. Your noun is Transport (step 11), which wraps whichever one and hides the wire.

L3 was three jobs. Only L3b swaps with the wire.

The headline: L3 wasn't one layer, it was three — the log/resume (Durable-Streams-shaped), the pipe (PartyKit), and your normalize seam (Transport). Only the pipe (5–9) changes when you move from SSE to WS, and PartyKit hands you that whole band — while the Durable-Streams-shaped log/resume (3·4·10) stays exactly the deferrable feature you can add later, untouched by the wire choice.
