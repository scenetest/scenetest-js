Q: a “partysse” — one PartyKit client for both wires?

No. PartySocket / PartyServer are WebSocket-only (per PartyKit docs). durable-streams/client is SSE-only. So no third-party lib spans both wires.

⇒ the thing that unifies dev+cloud is your Transport interface — one interface, two thin impls (EventSource · partysocket). You already have the SSE one.

Q: one spec for both legs & both wires?

Yes — and you already own it. The verb vocab (RowOp) and the envelope format (@scenetest/protocol / “jsonl line”) are wire-agnostic — same on SSE & WS, roll-up & roll-down.

Durable's State protocol is an optional standard diction for the verb layer; their Streams (wire) protocol is SSE-bound, so it can't be the shared keystone.

The collapse: the two feet are one shared thing (zod + TanStack DB); the verb + envelope layers are one shared spec you already wrote; TransportDB ⟷ StreamDB are the same role. So the entire base of the arch is wire-blind and built — and the keystone is the only dev/cloud delta, sealed behind the Transport seam. You don't need to adopt durable-streams to ship cloud — you need a partysocket Transport + a partyserver emitter at the keystone, and the base you already have just works underneath.
